# Farmer Persona

## Profile: Maria Rodriguez

**Demographics:**
- 45 years old
- Owner of a 300-head sheep farm in the Midwest
- 20+ years of livestock farming experience
- Associates degree in Animal Science
- Medium technical proficiency

**Goals:**
1. Access practical insights from animal genetics research to improve flock health and productivity
2. Track and analyze sheep performance data to make better breeding decisions
3. Participate in breeding programs to test new genetic lines
4. Reduce costs and increase sustainability of farming operations
5. Stay informed about relevant innovations in animal husbandry

**Pain Points:**
1. Research findings are often too technical and academic to apply practically
2. Limited time to search through and interpret scientific literature
3. Difficulty connecting with researchers who understand farm-level challenges
4. Inconsistent methods for tracking and analyzing animal performance data
5. Limited feedback channels for reporting results of new breeding techniques

**Technical Capabilities:**
- Comfortable with basic mobile and web applications
- Uses farm management software for operational tasks
- Limited experience with data analysis tools
- Prefers visual information and practical instructions
- Primarily accesses technology via smartphone and tablet

**Usage Scenarios:**
1. Checking recommendations based on flock health and breeding cycles
2. Uploading animal data and images for analysis and record-keeping
3. Communicating with researchers about genetic line performance
4. Reviewing simplified research findings relevant to sheep breeding
5. Accessing diagnostic assistance for animal health issues

**Key Features:**
- Mobile-friendly interface with offline capabilities
- Simplified data visualization dashboards
- Plain-language summaries of genetic research findings
- Direct communication channels with animal genetics researchers
- AI-assisted livestock diagnostics and breeding recommendations
