# Example Questions for Emilia

## Farmers

1. **Herd overview & composition**  
   "Show me how many animals I currently have on my farm, broken down by breed and sex, in a simple table and bar chart."

2. **Growth and weights (50/100 days)**  
   "For my lambs born this season, show a chart of adjusted 50‑day and 100‑day weights by sire, and highlight any sires whose lambs are below the flock average."

3. **Individual animal performance summary**  
   "Give me a one‑page summary for animal 12345: birth date, breed, growth metrics, ultrasound results, carcass records, and most recent genetic proofs."

4. **Culling and disposal analysis**  
   "List all animals culled in the last 12 months by disposal reason, and show a chart of the most common disposal codes on my farm."

5. **Milk production monitoring**  
   "Show me a line graph of total test‑day milk weight over time for each cow, and flag any animals whose milk weight has dropped more than 20% in the last three tests."

6. **Milk components (fat & protein)**  
   "Compare average milk fat% and protein% for my herd over the last year, and show which animals consistently have the highest and lowest component percentages."

7. **Breeding & pedigree planning**  
   "For my current replacement ewe lambs, show their dams and sires, and recommend which ones look like the best breeding candidates based on growth and genetic proofs."

8. **Health & ultrasound / carcass quality**  
   "Summarize ultrasound and carcass records for my finished lambs this year: average loin depth, fat depth, carcass weight, and ranking, and show which sires produced the top‑ranked carcasses."

9. **Scrapie genotype risk overview**  
   "Show the distribution of scrapie genotypes in my flock, and list any animals that should be prioritized for culling or monitoring based on their genotype."

10. **Farmer‑level benchmarking**  
    "Compare my farm’s average growth and carcass scores to the overall database averages for the same breeds, and show in a chart where my flock is above or below those benchmarks."

## Researchers

1. **PCA on traits**  
   "Run a PCA on growth and carcass traits for my 2023 lamb cohort and show a scatter plot of the first two components, coloured by breed."

2. **GWAS interpretation with internal data**  
   "Using our latest GWAS results for trait X, list the top SNPs for the main peak and explain how much variation they account for in my dataset."

3. **Heritability and proofs**  
   "Summarize the genetic proofs (GEBVs and accuracies) for growth traits in my rams, and show a table ranked by direct growth EBV with a bar chart."

4. **Correlations and trade‑offs**  
   "Compute correlations between growth, carcass, and fertility traits in my flock and highlight any strong antagonistic relationships in a correlation heatmap."

5. **Ultrasound & carcass quality**  
   "Compare ultrasound loin depth and carcass scores for animals with genetic proofs above and below the flock average, and visualize the differences."

6. **GWAS in livestock – literature only**  
   "What recent papers discuss GWAS for growth and carcass traits in sheep or closely related species? Summarize key findings and give me a citation list."

7. **Genetic parameters and heritability (literature)**  
   "Find recent literature estimating heritabilities and genetic correlations for fertility and lamb survival in sheep, and summarize the typical parameter ranges with citations."

8. **Heat stress and climate resilience (literature)**  
   "Retrieve the latest open‑access research on genetic resistance to heat stress in dairy or meat sheep, and explain which traits or markers are considered promising, with references."

9. **Genomic selection strategies (literature)**  
   "What does the literature say about genomic selection strategies for small flocks—minimum reference population size, accuracy, and cost–benefit? Provide a concise overview with cited papers."

10. **Scrapie genotype and breeding policy (literature)**  
    "Summarize current recommendations in the literature on using scrapie genotypes in breeding programs, including which genotypes are preferred or discouraged and why, with citations."
